Highlights

- Bearing support stiffness identified from axle-box acceleration in simulation
- Rail roughness spectrum levels are nuisance parameters; roughness is not measured
- Passes at different speeds separate the support resonance from the roughness
- Ignoring wheel-to-wheel coupling through the rail biases the stiffness by 5.6%
- Periods are compared within one payload band; mixed-payload pooling is not supported
